## Architecture Decision

One source-grounded short spoken explanation, not a general PTE simulator or personality/communication assessor.

1. Preserve original audio and a faithful timebase; quality/decode before scoring.
2. Run audio observations (VAD/quality/timing) and ASR/transcript preparation separately; join by original timestamps.
3. Correct only clearly identified transcription issues, retaining raw text and revision provenance. Unknown cannot be invented as confidence.
4. Content/logic analysis first receives source, confirmed criteria and reliable transcript, but no acoustic fluency statistics.
5. Delivery analysis receives transcript plus actual measured events; a controlled report joins outputs with references. Same model can handle separate requests; not an agent swarm or automatic multiple-provider setup.
6. Measurements, boundaries, excerpt locations and status remain program-owned. A model may interpret supported patterns, not alter durations, invent events or conclude that pauses prove lack of knowledge.
7. Original audio replay, actual selective re-record and saved history complete the functional version. Long-term learning/retention/payment are later validation, not claims from a local test pass.

A built-in checker contains statistical detectors as well as deterministic calculations. VAD output is estimated speech activity, not a psychological hesitation measurement. Norms require data; no universal pause cutoff or English fluency score applied to Mandarin knowledge.

## Specific Engineering Traps To Avoid

- Silero's inspected implementation filters short segments, pads speech boundaries and rounds optional seconds output. Preserve sample indices/config; calibrate assessment rather than copy slicing defaults.
- ASR may omit fillers/self-corrections or invert a critical word. A timestamped transcript is not certified truth; an alignment model cannot restore omitted words simply by matching them to a waveform.
- Recording gaps caused by user pause/device failure are not speech pauses; leading/trailing waiting is separate from internal breaks.
- No cutting silence/time-stretch before measuring, and any segmented decode must map to original clock.
- Mandarin character rate is not English WPM or syllables per second. Define numerator/denominator, segmentation and uncertainty.
- No-source or uncertain-source facts cannot justify negative OR positive mastery claims. A valid alternative formulation is not automatically wrong.
- Text correction invalidates dependent metrics/alignment/assessments but is not new speech performance; compare recordings under stable criteria/config.
- Bad model schema/reference must fail visibly or deliver partial results, not default success. Record actual usage/retries rather than fabricate latency/cost.
