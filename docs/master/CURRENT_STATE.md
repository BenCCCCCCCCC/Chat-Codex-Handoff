- F01 workspace/task creation and durable save/reopen;
- F02 source-linked editable/AI-proposed criteria and explicit approved revisions;

1. Non-engineering owner can complete a task without hand-authoring JSON/SHA/long prompts.

4. Results populate the product through completed files, not pasted final chat text.
